Quick Start

Get up and running in under 5 minutes.

  1. Plug in GarminBridge

    Connect via USB-C to your treadmill's built-in USB port, a wall adapter, or a power bank. The LED will turn blue while it searches for a treadmill.

  2. Power on your treadmill

    Turn on the treadmill and make sure it's in Bluetooth range (within ~10 m). GarminBridge scans automatically. The LED will turn yellow once it's connected to the treadmill.

    Using iFit, Kinomap, or Zwift? Disconnect those apps from the treadmill first. Only one Bluetooth device can connect as an FTMS central at a time.
  3. Add GarminBridge as a foot pod on your Garmin

    On your watch: Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Add New → Foot Pod. Select "GarminBridge" when it appears. The LED will turn green once the watch connects.

All set! Start a run activity on your Garmin. Speed and distance will come directly from the treadmill.

LED States

The single RGB LED on GarminBridge tells you exactly what's happening.

LED ColorMeaningWhat to do
Blue Booting or scanning for a treadmill Power on your treadmill and wait
Yellow Treadmill connected, waiting for watch Pair GarminBridge as a foot pod on your watch
Red Watch connected, no treadmill found Check the treadmill is on and in Bluetooth range. Disconnect any iFit/Kinomap apps.
Green Fully operational Start your run — speed and distance are flowing to the watch

Pairing with Garmin

The pairing steps are the same across all Garmin running watches.

  1. Make sure GarminBridge is powered on and connected to the treadmill (yellow or green LED).
  2. On your Garmin watch, navigate to Menu → Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Add New.
  3. Select Foot Pod and wait for the watch to scan. "GarminBridge" will appear in the list.
  4. Select it. The watch will confirm pairing. The LED on GarminBridge will turn green.
  5. Start a Run or Treadmill Run activity. Speed and distance now come from GarminBridge.
Other foot pods interfering? If you have a real running foot pod already paired, disable or remove it first. Garmin watches typically use the most recently paired foot pod.

Re-pairing after a firmware update

The nRF52840's Bluetooth address is derived from its factory identity key and does not change between firmware updates. You should not need to re-pair after a firmware update. If the watch can't find GarminBridge, delete the sensor from the watch (Settings → Sensors → GarminBridge → Remove) and pair again.

How to check your treadmill

Your treadmill supports FTMS if it:

  • Works with Zwift, Kinomap, or RGT Cycling via Bluetooth
  • Has a "Bluetooth" or "Smart" designation in its model name
  • Connects to an app (iFit, Peloton, etc.) and that connection is via Bluetooth (not Wi-Fi only)

Not Compatible

  • Peloton Tread — closed proprietary Bluetooth protocol
  • LifeFitness commercial machines — proprietary protocol
  • Concept2 SkiErg / BikeErg — CSAFE protocol (not FTMS)
  • Manual/non-motorized treadmills — no Bluetooth electronics

Speed & Distance Accuracy

GarminBridge forwards speed and distance data exactly as broadcast by the treadmill. It does not add or remove any data — the accuracy is purely a function of your treadmill's own calibration.

Most consumer treadmills are accurate to within ±2–5% of stated speed. If your treadmill reads 10.0 km/h, that's exactly what your Garmin will record.

No calibration is required on GarminBridge or your watch.

Distance

Distance comes from the treadmill's odometer (FTMS Total Distance field). It is accumulated by the treadmill, not by GarminBridge. If you pause and restart a run, the distance counter depends on how your treadmill handles that scenario.

Cadence

GarminBridge deliberately transmits a cadence of 0 steps/min in the RSC packet.

This is a design choice: the Seeed XIAO nRF52840 has no accelerometer, so it cannot count steps. Sending 0 tells your Garmin to use its own cadence measurement (wrist-based optical cadence or foot-pod accelerometer) rather than an incorrect value from GarminBridge.

On Garmin watches that support wrist-based cadence (most Forerunner and Fenix models), your cadence will still be recorded accurately from the watch's own motion sensors.

Note: The RSC Feature characteristic (0x2A54) is set to only advertise "Total Distance Supported" (bit 1). Stride Length and Instantaneous Cadence features are not advertised, which signals to the watch that it should not rely on GarminBridge for those metrics.

Inclination / Grade

GarminBridge reads inclination (grade) from the treadmill via the FTMS Treadmill Data characteristic when the treadmill provides it. The value is available in the web analyzer and serial debug output.

However, the RSC foot pod protocol (0x2A53) has no inclination field. This means GarminBridge cannot forward inclination to your watch — it is a protocol limitation, not a firmware limitation.

Your Garmin watch uses its own barometric altimeter to track elevation gain. On most treadmills, the treadmill deck does not physically rise with inclination, so Garmin's altimeter will correctly record 0 elevation gain even when the belt is inclined.

Future firmware versions may add a custom BLE characteristic to expose inclination to the web analyzer and potentially to Garmin Connect IQ data fields.

Firmware Updates

GarminBridge uses the UF2 bootloader built into the Adafruit nRF52 Arduino BSP. Updates are drag-and-drop simple.

Check your current version

Connect via USB and open the Arduino Serial Monitor (115200 baud). GarminBridge will print its version on startup when #define DEBUG 1 is set.

Update via UF2 (recommended)

  1. Double-tap the reset button on GarminBridge rapidly. The LED will flash and a USB drive named XIAO-BOOT will appear on your computer.
  2. Download the latest .uf2 file from the GitHub Releases page.
  3. Drag the .uf2 file onto the XIAO-BOOT drive. The device reboots automatically.

This method works on any OS (Windows, macOS, Linux) without any special software.

Or use the web updater for guided instructions and WebUSB bootloader trigger.

Troubleshooting

GarminBridge not found by my watch

GarminBridge only advertises as an RSC peripheral when active. Make sure:

  • GarminBridge is powered on (any LED color means it's running)
  • Your watch is in "Add New Sensor" mode when scanning
  • No other device is already connected to GarminBridge as a central (another phone/watch pairing session)
Speed shows as ~39% of actual speed

This is a bug in firmware versions before v1.1.0. The RSC speed unit is 1/256 m/s, but old firmware used 1/100 m/s — causing a 2.56× underreading (100/256 ≈ 39%).

Update your firmware via the updater page to fix this immediately.

LED stays Blue — no treadmill found
  • Make sure the treadmill is powered on and in Bluetooth range (<10 m)
  • Confirm the treadmill supports FTMS (see Compatible Treadmills)
  • Disconnect any other apps connected to the treadmill (iFit, Kinomap, Zwift) — only one device can be the FTMS central
  • Try power-cycling GarminBridge (unplug and replug USB)
LED stays Yellow — watch won't connect
  • Make sure you've added GarminBridge as a foot pod via Settings → Sensors → Add New
  • Try removing any old foot pod sensors from the watch and re-pairing
  • Keep the watch within 3–5 m of GarminBridge during pairing
  • Some Garmin models require starting a run activity before the foot pod connects
Treadmill already connected to iFit/Kinomap

FTMS only allows a single "central" Bluetooth connection at a time. If iFit or another app is connected, GarminBridge cannot connect. You must choose one or the other.

On your phone/tablet: close the fitness app completely (force-quit it, don't just minimize). GarminBridge will auto-connect to the treadmill within a few seconds.

Watch shows speed but distance stays at 0

Some treadmills do not broadcast Total Distance in the FTMS Treadmill Data characteristic. This is a treadmill limitation — GarminBridge can only forward what the treadmill sends.

Open the web analyzer to inspect the raw FTMS packets and see exactly what your treadmill is broadcasting.

GarminBridge disconnects from the treadmill mid-run

This can happen if:

  • The treadmill goes out of Bluetooth range
  • The treadmill's Bluetooth radio resets (some models do this when speed changes significantly)
  • Another device briefly connects to the treadmill

GarminBridge will automatically resume scanning and reconnect when the treadmill is available again. The LED will go back to Blue briefly, then Yellow/Green once reconnected.

Speed is correct but cadence shows 0

This is expected. See Cadence for a full explanation. Your watch is measuring cadence with its own sensors, which is more accurate than what GarminBridge could provide.

Privacy

GarminBridge is a standalone Bluetooth bridge. It has no internet connection, makes no network requests, and collects no data of any kind.

All activity data flows directly from your treadmill to your watch via Bluetooth. None of it passes through any server, cloud service, or third party.

This website (the docs, analyzer, and updater pages) also collects no data, uses no cookies, and has no analytics.
